Article figures & evidence still needed
  1. Corpus. Four named buildings, dated/source-attributed photographs, measured plans and selected bays. Building identities are still to be confirmed.
  2. Geometric extraction. Reference versus generated plan and section at matched scale; identify springing points, ribs, seed triangles and symmetry.
  3. Recursive grammar. Edge evaluation, signed vertical movement, four child faces and A/B/C inheritance; show seed + five passes for each case.
  4. Validation. Compare corresponding vertices, rib junctions and profiles against measured models. A Rhino rule fit is not historical validation.
  5. Design space. The 4 × 6 matrix with parameter values and rule glyphs, separating calibrated baselines from speculative permutations.
