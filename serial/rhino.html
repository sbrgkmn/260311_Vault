Method and reference fidelity

Tierceron II uses the recovered Rhino seed and directed subdivision connectivity. Its all-A default reproduces the measured length-based rule. Edited rules produce new studies. Quadripartite, sexpartite, tierceron and stellar presets use distinct procedural seeds, not verified reconstructions of the reference models.

Four mirrored quarters form each bay. Every face splits into four children; family assignments determine subsequent development. Colours show the families assigned to the selected generation; they act on its edges in the next pass.

Rhino objects are not explicitly named Tierceron_2. That title remains a visual attribution. Original files and earlier studies are retained on disk.
