The modelling argument

1 / Simplify the source

Identify springers, ridge targets and connected regions. Construct flat triangles rather than fitting ribs onto a generic rounded roof.

2 / Develop the triangle

Evaluate shared edges, displace the new points vertically and inherit face rules. Plan and 3D geometry come from the same recursive mesh.

3 / Compare, then fit

Reflect the developed quarter into a bay. Fit landmarks and heights before claiming accuracy. Triangle density is not a similarity metric.

Research limits and next validation

The quadrant folds and first plan divisions are source-informed abstractions. Bristol uses recursive face centres; the other families develop their initial plan constraints and then subdivide edges. The Wells reference is a single bay containing multiple compartments. Generated reference plans are marked in the atlas. No source-fitting error has yet been measured. Lincoln retains a full-bay stagger; Westminster's corner fans remain height-field abstractions.

Next: register source landmarks, fit seed proportions and heights, then measure error through recursion. Image permissions for the paper must be verified separately from local research use.
